# chanceify™

A small, fast Spotify player for your desktop. It plays your Spotify, shows
your music stats, and keeps everything it saves in one folder next to the app.
Nothing is sent anywhere except to Spotify and, if you turn it on, Last.fm.

You need a Spotify **Premium** account.

## Get it

1. Go to the [releases page](https://github.com/madlygeeked/chanceify/releases).
2. Download `chanceify.exe`.
3. Put it in its own folder and run it. That folder is where it keeps its settings.
4. Sign in with Spotify.
