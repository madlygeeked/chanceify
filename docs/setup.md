# Connect your accounts

## Your own Spotify app (optional, Premium)

This gives you your own private connection to Spotify, which avoids shared limits.

1. Open the [Spotify developer dashboard](https://developer.spotify.com/dashboard) and sign in.
2. Click **Create app**. Any name works.
3. Under **Redirect URIs** add exactly `http://127.0.0.1:8989/login`
4. Tick **Web API**, accept the terms, save.
5. Copy the **Client ID**.
6. In chanceify open **Settings, Personal Spotify app**, paste it and click **Authorize**.

## Last.fm

1. Open **Settings, Last.fm** and click **Connect**.
2. Your browser opens Last.fm. Click **Yes, allow access**.
3. Come back to chanceify. Your plays start to scrobble.
