# what it does

- **plays your spotify.** library, playlists, queue, search, lyrics, connect devices.
- **music stats.** top artists and songs for 7 days, 30 days or all time. a streak. a recap picture to share.
- **last.fm.** scrobbling, love, and missing cover art saved for you.
- **looks.** 16 themes, your own colours, colours that follow the album cover.
- **visualizer.** bars, flow and swirl. mix them. full screen, in an extra window, or behind the lyrics.
- **views.** right-click the round disc to switch parts of the screen on or off. save your view.
- **mini player.** its own small window.
- **calm mode.** a slow ocean, the song name and one play button.
- **keys.** every key can be changed.
- **your files stay with you.** everything lives in the folder beside the app.
