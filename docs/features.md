# What it does

- **Plays your Spotify.** Library, playlists, queue, search, lyrics, Connect devices.
- **Music stats.** Top artists and songs for 7 days, 30 days or all time, a listening streak, and a recap picture you can save and share.
- **Last.fm.** Scrobbling, love, and missing cover art saved for you.
- **Looks.** 16 themes, your own colours, colours that follow the album cover, and several visualizers.
- **Keys.** Every key can be changed. Try a ready-made set, export, import, or save as the default.
- **Views.** Right-click the round disc to open a floating panel that switches every part of the screen on or off.
- **Your files stay with you.** Everything is saved in the folder beside the app.

- The visualizer settings panel can be dragged to any size. In full-screen visualizer, bars on all four edges have the same thickness and meet cleanly in the corners.
- Full-screen lyrics has the same play buttons and song-length bar as the normal view, in a box under the album art.

## Extra visualizer window (0.77.0)
Views panel > "New visualizer window" opens one extra window that shows the visualizer with the same settings as the main window. Drag it to another monitor and double-click (or F11) for full screen. Esc leaves full screen, then closes it. The End key (rebindable) or the same Views button closes it from the main window. Only one extra window, to keep the drawing cost down.
