# Keyboard shortcuts

Press **T** (or open **Keyboard shortcuts** from the menu under your picture)
to see every key. Click a key to change it, right-click to clear it. The list
floats, so you can still move and resize the window while it is open.

You can export your keys to a file, import them again, or save them as the
default for everyone.

Starting keys:

| Key | Does |
|-----|------|
| Esc | Leave full screen and go to the top of the playlist that is playing |
| X | Show the queue and lyrics together (press again to close both) |
| V | Full-screen visualizer |
| B | Visualizer settings |
| C | Full-screen lyrics |
| Z | Views |
| M | Mini player |
| T | This list |

In the shortcuts list (press T) click a key to change it. Shift and Ctrl can go with any key, for example Ctrl+Up for volume up or Ctrl+Z for undo. Right-click a key to clear it.

[Back](./)

**Click to queue.** Ctrl+click a song (a row, or a tile on Home) to add it to the queue without opening or playing anything. In the shortcuts list (T) the "Queue a song" button switches it to Alt+click or off. While Ctrl is the queue key, Alt+click picks several rows.

**Play next.** Shift+Ctrl+click (or Shift+Alt+click) a song row puts it at the top of the queue. The "Tap tempo" key is now "Bass jump": press it on the beat and the screen flashes and covers that bounce to the bass jump.

**Rebinding.** Press Ctrl or Shift on its own and the box keeps waiting; only a real key finishes the bind. Under Playback, "Queue the song under the pointer" and "Queue the song under the pointer, to play next" can be put on any key (G, say). Ctrl+click and Shift+Ctrl+click always do the same.

## Views and calm mode (0.76.0)
- ` (backtick/tilde): back to the default view. Rebindable.
- Home: calm mode. A slow ocean, the song name and one small play button. Nothing reacts to the music. Home or Esc comes back. Rebindable.
